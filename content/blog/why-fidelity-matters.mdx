File conversion is often treated as a commodity—a simple background task that "just works." However, as digital experiences become more visual and performance-driven, the **fidelity** of these conversions has moved from a nice-to-have to a critical business requirement.

## The Cost of Low-Quality Conversions

When an image is compressed or a document is converted using suboptimal libraries, you risk:
1. **Visual Artifacts:** Distracting noise that devalues your brand.
2. **Color Inaccuracy:** Breaking brand guidelines or misrepresenting products.
3. **Bloated File Sizes:** Paradoxically, poor algorithms often result in larger files that perform worse.
